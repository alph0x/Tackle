## 3. Applying this edition

For customers who price from this tariff:

1. Change the per-pallet handling rate in your own systems before the edition comes into force.
2. Check it against a known amount: under edition 2026-11, 10 pallets cost 19150 cents in handling.
3. Mark the copy of this tariff that you keep: add a line `applied: YYYY-MM-DD` (the date you made the
   change) directly under the first line of the copy, so anyone reading it can tell which edition your
   prices follow.

Questions about this tariff: the Northgate account desk.
