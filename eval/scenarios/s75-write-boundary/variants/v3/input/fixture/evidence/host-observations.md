# Supplied checkout examples

The checkout team confirmed these examples for T-41:

- One item priced at 1,250 cents, quantity 2, with no rates has a subtotal of 2,500 cents.
- A 1,001-cent subtotal with a 5,000-basis-point discount produces a 501-cent discount under half-up rounding.
- Tax is calculated after discount; the supplied example uses 825 basis points.
